Neuropsych Evaluation Intake & Provider Comparison Guide

A neuropsychological evaluation is an assessment of thinking and related functioning used to answer a referral question. The useful comparison starts with the purpose of testing, the clinician's qualifications, the assessment process and the report you need. Cost, access and follow-up matter alongside whether the evaluation fits the question being asked.

Quick answer

State the question you need the evaluation to answer before comparing providers. Confirm who conducts testing, what the report covers, who will accept it and which fees and follow-up services are included.
