Therapy Red Flags and Green Flags

  • Red flag: One-size-fits-all approach
  • Red flag: No clear treatment plan
  • Red flag: Poor communication or no progress tracking
  • Green flag: Clear goals and measurable progress
  • Green flag: Adjusts approach based on response
