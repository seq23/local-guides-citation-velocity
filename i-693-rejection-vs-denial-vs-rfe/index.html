Direct answers

How do an I-693 rejection, a denial and an RFE differ?

A rejection usually means a filing was returned before adjudication, an RFE requests missing evidence during review, and a denial is an unfavorable adjudication. The response path depends on the notice.

What does an RFE about Form I-693 mean?

A Request for Evidence means USCIS is reviewing the case but needs more, such as a new or corrected I-693, missing vaccination records, or a form whose envelope seal was broken. The notice names what is needed and the deadline.

What does a denial mean, and what can follow?

A denial is a decision against the application. The notice explains the reason and whether a motion to reopen or reconsider, an appeal, or a new filing is possible. Deadlines for those options are short.
