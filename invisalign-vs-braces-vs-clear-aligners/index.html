Direct answers

How do Invisalign, braces and other clear aligners differ?

Braces provide fixed orthodontic control; clear aligners are removable and depend heavily on case fit and wear compliance. Brand name alone does not determine suitability.

When do clear aligners fit?

Clear aligners fit mild to moderate crowding or spacing in people who will wear the trays most of the day and keep them clean. Invisalign is one brand; other aligner brands work in a similar way with different supervision models.

When do braces fit better?

Braces fit complex tooth movement, bite problems and cases where wearing removable trays would be hard. Because they stay on, they keep working without daily effort from the patient.
