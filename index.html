Questions about The Industry Guides

Who runs this site?
The Industry Guides is an independent editorial publisher covering six regulated service categories.
How are guides verified?
Pages use named frameworks, visible source records, review dates, and a hard publication gate.
How fresh is the data?
Every page carries a source-derived modification date. Regulated claims are scheduled for recheck.
Is this a government agency?
No. We link to primary government and professional sources where they control the rule.

About The Industry Guides

The Industry Guides is an independent editorial publisher of source-backed decision guides for regulated and high-stakes services: USCIS medical exams, personal injury, dentistry, neuropsychological testing, hormone, IV and hair treatment, and senior living. Each guide explains the rules, compares the factors that matter, and routes you to a provider when you are ready.
