Dentist vs oral surgeon vs endodontist

A general dentist is the provider who manages broad oral care; an oral surgeon is a specialist who handles surgical conditions of the mouth and jaw, and an endodontist is a specialist in dental pulp and root-canal treatment. The diagnosis determines the right provider, so confirm it before booking a specialist.

Direct answers

How do a general dentist, an oral surgeon and an endodontist differ?

A general dentist manages broad oral care, an oral surgeon handles surgical conditions, and an endodontist focuses on dental pulp and root-canal treatment. The diagnosis determines the right provider.

When is a general dentist the right first stop?

A general dentist is the right first stop for checkups, cavities, chipped teeth and most tooth pain. They diagnose the problem, treat many cases themselves and refer to a specialist when the case needs more.

When do I need an oral surgeon or an endodontist?

See an oral surgeon for impacted wisdom teeth, complex extractions, implants or jaw problems. See an endodontist for a difficult root canal, a retreatment, or tooth pain whose source is hard to find.
