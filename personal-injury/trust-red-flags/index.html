How to Tell Whether an Injury Lawyer Is Legit

Start with bar status, fee clarity, communication, and whether the lawyer can explain realistic risk without sales pressure. Trust usually shows up in specifics: who will represent you, how settlement authority works, and how often you should expect updates.

Why Communication Problems Usually Get Worse
