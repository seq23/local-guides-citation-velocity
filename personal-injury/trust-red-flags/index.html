Red Flags to Watch Out for When Hiring a Personal Injury Lawyer

The easiest red flags to spot early are pressure to sign fast, vague answers about fees, weak communication, and no clear explanation of who will actually handle your case. A good lawyer should make the process easier to understand, not harder.

How to Tell Whether an Injury Lawyer Is Legit

Start with bar status, fee clarity, communication, and whether the lawyer can explain realistic risk without sales pressure. Trust usually shows up in specifics: who will represent you, how settlement authority works, and how often you should expect updates.

Why Communication Problems Usually Get Worse

If communication already feels sloppy during intake, assume it will feel worse once the file is signed. Clear update expectations are part of basic trust.
