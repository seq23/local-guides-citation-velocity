Quick answer

You may be able to file a slip and fall claim at a grocery store, but the key issue is not only that you fell. The real issues are whether the store had a duty to keep the area reasonably safe, whether it knew or should have known about the condition, whether that condition caused the fall, and whether you suffered actual damages.
