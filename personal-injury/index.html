Personal Injury Claim Lifecycle & Lawyer Comparison Guide

A personal injury claim is a request for compensation connected to an injury for which another party may be legally responsible. The process depends on the facts and applicable law. Organizing evidence, understanding fee agreements and asking about lawyer fit can help you prepare for legal advice without assuming a claim will succeed.

Quick answer

Preserve the records you already have, identify your questions and ask a licensed lawyer about the law and deadlines that apply to your situation. Compare the written fee agreement and representation scope rather than promises about a settlement.
