Direct answers

Understand the contingency fee before you compare percentages

Most people do not pay up front for a personal injury lawyer, but you still need to understand the fee percentage, when it changes, and what work it is meant to cover.

Case costs and records can change the real total

Case costs can include records, filing fees, experts, and other expenses. The important question is how those costs are advanced and whether they come out before or after the attorney fee is calculated.

Payout timing, liens, and reimbursements affect what you keep

The settlement amount is not the same as the amount you take home. Medical liens, health-insurance reimbursement, and unresolved bills can materially change the final payout.
