What Questions Should I Ask During A Free Consultation With A Personal Injury Attorney

A useful consultation is not just “Do you take my case?” The goal is to compare experience, strategy, communication, timing, and fee handling in a way that lets you stack answers side by side across firms.

What Should I Ask A Lawyer Before Signing A Contingency Agreement

Before signing, you want clarity on who runs the file, how the fee changes if the case becomes harder, what costs are separate, how updates work, and what the first major milestones will be. That is how you avoid comparing firms on charm alone.

What Should I Ask If Two Injury Lawyers Give Different Advice

Different advice is common because lawyers may see liability, value, timing, and risk differently. The way to compare is asking each lawyer to explain where the disagreement actually is and what evidence would change their view.
