How to compare local personal injury lawyers safely

Direct answer

For “How to compare local personal injury lawyers safely”: do not compare lawyers on slogans like "best" or "top." Compare them on the same checklist every time: accident-type fit, local fit, who will actually manage the file, fee structure, communication quality, and whether they explain the next 30 days clearly. A lawyer who explains the process plainly usually tells you more than a lawyer who only sells confidence.
