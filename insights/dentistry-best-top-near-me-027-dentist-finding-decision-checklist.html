Quick checklist

  • Treatment fit: routine, pediatric, implant, cosmetic, emergency, or sedation need
  • Credentials: license, specialty training, procedure volume, and referral patterns
  • Reviews: look for explanation quality, follow-up, billing clarity, and outcomes
  • Insurance/cost: written estimate, payment options, and what is included
  • Access: emergency slots, after-hours instructions, and realistic appointment timing

Red flags to watch

  • Only “top rated” claims with no treatment-specific detail
  • No written estimate or treatment sequence
  • Won’t explain alternatives, risks, or when to get a second opinion
