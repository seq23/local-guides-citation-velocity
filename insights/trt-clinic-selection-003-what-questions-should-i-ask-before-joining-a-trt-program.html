What Questions Should I Ask Before Joining A Trt Program?

A TRT program is an arrangement for clinical assessment, treatment when appropriate and ongoing follow-up related to testosterone care. Programs differ in the services and fees they describe. Before joining, separate the medical decision from the subscription terms and ask who is responsible for diagnosis, monitoring, prescriptions and questions between appointments.

Quick answer

Ask who diagnoses and treats you, what assessment and monitoring include, how fertility plans are discussed and what the written fees cover. Confirm follow-up access and cancellation terms before paying for a program.

What should I check before deciding?

  • Identify the clinician responsible for your care.
  • Ask about monitoring, fertility discussions and follow-up.
  • Compare included services, exclusions and cancellation terms.
