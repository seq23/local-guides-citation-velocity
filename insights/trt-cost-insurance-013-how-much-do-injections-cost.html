How much do injections cost?

Direct answer

For “How much do injections cost”: tRT cost is easier to compare when you break it into a cost-by-method table instead of chasing one headline number. Injections are often the lowest monthly medication cost but still add labs and follow-up visits; gels can cost more monthly but may feel simpler; pellets may front-load cost and procedure fees. Always compare insurance coverage, out-of-pocket cost, startup labs, repeat labs, follow-ups, and realistic.

Quick checklist

  • Injections: compare monthly medication cost, supplies, repeat labs, and follow-up visit fees
  • Gels: compare monthly medication price, insurance coverage, refill friction, and follow-up costs
  • Pellets: compare insertion fee, repeat procedure timing, monitoring cost, and total annual cost

  • Annual cost: add startup workup, repeat labs, medication, visits, and any add-on fees
