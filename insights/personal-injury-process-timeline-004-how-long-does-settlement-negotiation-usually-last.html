How Long Does Settlement Negotiation Usually Last

Direct answer

For “How Long Does Settlement Negotiation Usually Last”: settlement negotiation length depends on whether treatment is complete, records are organized, liability is clear, and policy information is known. Some negotiations move quickly once the file is mature, but others drag because the insurer disputes value, waits on records, or uses time pressure to force a smaller number.
