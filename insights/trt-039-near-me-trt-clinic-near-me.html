Quick checklist

  • Monitoring: what labs are checked, when, and what changes the plan
  • Pricing: startup cost, monthly cost, refill policy, and extra lab fees
  • Lab frequency: baseline, early follow-up, and steady-state monitoring schedule
  • Fertility guidance: whether sperm banking, HCG, or family-planning goals are discussed before treatment
  • Follow-up policy: how side effects, dose changes, and missed labs are handled

Red flags to watch

  • Starts treatment before reviewing baseline labs
  • Quotes one number but cannot explain total annual cost
  • No fertility discussion when family-planning still matters
  • No clear follow-up policy for side effects or dose changes
  • Promises fast results without monitoring
