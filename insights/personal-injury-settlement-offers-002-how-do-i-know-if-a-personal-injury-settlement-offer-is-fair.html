How Do I Know If A Personal Injury Settlement Offer Is Fair?

A personal injury settlement offer is a proposed agreement to resolve a claim on stated terms. Its usefulness depends on the evidence, losses, legal responsibility and what rights the agreement releases. A headline payment alone does not explain the result after fees, expenses or other obligations connected with the claim.

Quick answer

Review the offer, release terms and supporting evidence with a licensed lawyer before agreeing. Ask for an explanation of the proposed amount, unresolved losses, deductions and rights you would give up; there is no reliable universal settlement number.

What this guide is best for: preparing questions about How Do I Know If A Personal Injury Settlement Offer Is Fair before making a decision.

What should I check before deciding?

  • Request the offer and proposed release in writing.
  • Review documented losses and unresolved treatment questions.
  • Ask how fees, expenses and other obligations affect the result.

What should I know about what this answer is based on?

A fair offer is not just a number that feels large. It has to line up with the injury story, the records, the wage loss, the future downside, and the leverage in the liability picture. The useful move is checking whether the offer accounts for the whole claim, not just the easiest line items.
