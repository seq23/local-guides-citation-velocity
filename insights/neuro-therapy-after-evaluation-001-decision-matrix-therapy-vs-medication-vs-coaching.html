Quick answer

Quick answer for Decision matrix: therapy vs medication vs coaching

Think in terms of severity, impairment, and support need. Therapy is often strongest when emotional regulation, habits, anxiety, or family systems are part of the picture. Medication is often considered when symptoms are impairing daily function and diagnostic clarity is strong. Coaching is often best as a practical support layer for follow-through and organization, not as a substitute for treatment when symptoms are severe.
