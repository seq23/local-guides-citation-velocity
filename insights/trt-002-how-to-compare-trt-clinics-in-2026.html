Important context

How to compare TRT clinics in 2026

Compare TRT clinics on four things that are verifiable before you pay: who prescribes and what their credentials are, what baseline testing is required before a prescription is issued, how monitoring is scheduled afterwards, and what the total cost is including labs. Telehealth clinics vary most on the first two — a clinic that will prescribe without a baseline blood panel and a documented clinical assessment is the clearest disqualifier. Ask every clinic the same set of questions and compare the answers side by side.

Copy-ready script

What to ask a telehealth TRT clinic before paying

Ask these by email so the answers are in writing and directly comparable between clinics.

  1. Who will write my prescription, what is their licence type, and in which state are they licensed?
  2. Which blood panel is required before a prescription can be issued?
  3. If my baseline result is out of range, do you repeat the test before starting treatment?
  4. What is the monitoring schedule after I start, and what is tested at each point?
  5. Are labs included in the price, or billed separately? What do they cost?
  6. Which pharmacy dispenses, and is the medication compounded or commercially manufactured?
  7. What is the total cost for twelve months including everything?
  8. How do I cancel, and what is the discontinuation plan if I stop?
Protocol

How to run the comparison

The point is to make clinics comparable, which marketing pages are designed to prevent.

  1. Shortlist three clinics rather than comparing one against nothing.
  2. Send the identical question set to all three, in writing.
  3. Record answers in a single table using the criteria above.
  4. Treat any unanswered question as a negative, not as neutral.
  5. Compare total 12-month cost, never monthly headline price.
  6. Discuss the shortlist with your own physician before committing, particularly if you have cardiovascular risk factors or are planning a family.
