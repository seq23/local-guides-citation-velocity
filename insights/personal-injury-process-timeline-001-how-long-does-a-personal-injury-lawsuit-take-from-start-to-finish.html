Quick answer for How Long Does a Personal Injury Lawsuit Take From Start to Finish?

A personal injury case is easier to understand as phases instead of one single number. Intake and investigation may take days to a few weeks, treatment and case development can take weeks or months, pre-suit negotiation can take weeks to months, and litigation can add many more months if the case does not settle early.
