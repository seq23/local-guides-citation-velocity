Do I need a referral?

Direct answer

Referral rules are not universal. A referral may be required by an insurance plan, a provider, or a specific evaluation pathway; self-referral may be accepted in other cases. Confirm with both the insurer and the evaluating practice before booking. The plan, provider, or program requires one.
