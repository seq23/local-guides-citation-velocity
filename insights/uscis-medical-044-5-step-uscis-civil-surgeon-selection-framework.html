5-step USCIS civil surgeon selection framework

Direct answer

For “5-step USCIS civil surgeon selection framework”: use a five-step selection framework before booking: confirm the doctor is a USCIS-designated civil surgeon, ask what the quoted price includes, verify vaccine and lab handling, confirm how the sealed I-693 is delivered or corrected, and choose the clinic that gives the clearest written next step. Then use uscisexam.com for local city pages and booking-specific guidance. Quick checklist

  • Confirm USCIS civil-surgeon designation before scheduling
  • Ask for the all-in price and what vaccines, labs, or follow-up visits cost extra
  • Ask what documents to bring and whether vaccine records must be translated
  • Ask how sealed I-693 pickup, mailing, and correction requests are handled

  • Clinic cannot explain the sealed-envelope process
  • Price excludes common vaccine or lab add-ons but is marketed as complete
  • Staff cannot explain what happens if the I-693 has an error
