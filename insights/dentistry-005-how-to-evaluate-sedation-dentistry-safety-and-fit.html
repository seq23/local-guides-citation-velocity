Quick checklist

  • Name the sedation type and who administers it
  • Ask what monitoring is used during the visit
  • Review medical history, medications, and contraindications first
  • Confirm recovery, escort, food/drink, and driving instructions
  • Separate sedation fees from procedure and follow-up costs
