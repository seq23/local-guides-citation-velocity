Quick checklist

  • Ask whether the issue looks hormonal, aesthetic, or mixed
  • Ask why PRP is being compared with microneedling
  • Ask what signs point toward labs, medications, or procedures first
  • Ask what timeline matters before changing the plan
  • Ask what evidence would change the recommendation

Red flags to watch

  • Jumps straight to a package without diagnosis logic
  • Treats PRP and microneedling like identical options
  • Cannot explain whether TRT could be relevant to the hair issue
  • Bundles hormonal and aesthetic paths into one sales pitch
  • No clarity on follow-up or maintenance expectations
