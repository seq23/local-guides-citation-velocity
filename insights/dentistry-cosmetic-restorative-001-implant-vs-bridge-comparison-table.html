Quick checklist

  • Implant: stand-alone tooth replacement when bone/support and candidacy fit
  • Bridge: uses neighboring teeth and may be faster in some cases
  • Cost: compare full treatment plan, not headline price
  • Timeline: implants can require staged care; bridges may be faster
  • Eligibility: imaging, gum health, neighboring teeth, and medical history matter
