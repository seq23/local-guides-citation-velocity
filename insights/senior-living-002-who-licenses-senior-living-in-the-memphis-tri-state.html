What this answer is based on

Tennessee communities are licensed by the Tennessee Health Facilities Commission; DeSoto County, Mississippi communities by the Mississippi State Department of Health; and Crittenden County, Arkansas communities by the Arkansas DHS Office of Long Term Care.
