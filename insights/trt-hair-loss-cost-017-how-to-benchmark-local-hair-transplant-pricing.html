Quick checklist

  • Compare annual cost, not just first-session price
  • Separate products that support regrowth from services that mainly improve appearance
  • Ask what maintenance cost looks like after the first 3 to 6 months
  • Ask what evidence supports the treatment for your pattern of loss
  • Ask when the clinic expects visible change and what counts as failure

Red flags to watch

  • Cosmetic pitch presented as hair-loss treatment
  • No annual cost framing
  • No maintenance discussion
  • No evidence explanation
  • Promises visible results on an unrealistic timeline
