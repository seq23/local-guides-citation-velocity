How do I get a replacement envelope?

A replacement Form I-693 envelope is a clinic-issued sealed package containing immigration medical examination documentation. Replacing or correcting that package is different from completing a new examination or establishing whether an earlier form remains usable. Contact the civil surgeon’s office and check current USCIS instructions for your filing situation before relying on an older copy.

Quick answer

Contact the civil surgeon’s office that prepared your Form I-693 and explain why you need a replacement. Ask how it handles a sealed copy, corrections and any fee, and check current USCIS instructions before filing.

What this guide is best for: preparing questions about How to get a replacement envelope before making a decision.

What should I check before deciding?

  • Explain whether the envelope was lost, opened or contains an error.
  • Ask the issuing clinic about replacement records and sealing.
  • Check USCIS instructions for your current filing situation.
