Direct answers

How do a neuropsych evaluation, a psychological evaluation and an IQ test differ?

A neuropsych evaluation examines patterns across cognitive domains, a psychological evaluation focuses more broadly on emotional and behavioral questions, and an IQ test is one instrument rather than a complete evaluation.

When is a neuropsych evaluation the right choice?

A neuropsych evaluation is the right choice when the concern involves memory, attention, language or problem solving linked to a medical or neurological condition, or when earlier testing left questions unanswered.

When is a psychological evaluation or an IQ test enough?

A psychological evaluation can be enough when the questions are about mood, anxiety, behavior or personality. An IQ test alone fits when a program or school needs one ability score, but it does not explain why someone struggles.
