# Velocity Best/Top Page Policy

Allowed: evaluation-only best/top pages, evidence-backed unranked shortlists, alphabetical shortlists, category-grouped shortlists, sourced comparisons, attributed review analysis, and public selection methodology.

Blocked: invented scores, ordinal rankings, arbitrary #1 claims, universal superiority claims, fake reviews, undisclosed sponsored ordering, and LLM-opinion-only rankings.
