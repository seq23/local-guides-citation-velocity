Direct answers

What is the core difference between a personal injury claim and a workers compensation claim?

A personal injury claim generally depends on another party’s legal responsibility; workers compensation is an employment-based benefit system with different proof, deadlines, and remedies.

When does a personal injury claim apply?

A personal injury claim applies when someone else's carelessness caused the injury, such as a car crash, a fall on another's property, or a defective product. You must show fault, and state deadlines apply.

When does workers compensation apply?

Workers compensation applies when an employee is hurt on the job or gets sick from work. It usually pays regardless of fault, but in exchange the worker generally cannot sue the employer for that injury.
