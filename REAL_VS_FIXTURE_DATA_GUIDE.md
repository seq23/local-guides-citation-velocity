# REAL VS FIXTURE DATA GUIDE

- Raw Twin Agent artifacts committed under `data/report_fixes/agent_runs/**` are historical/source artifacts and must be treated as immutable evidence.
- Repo-local public-signal/query data may be real captured input, normalized evidence, or deterministic planning intelligence; its proof boundary is declared in each contract/ledger.
- The 100K fanout dataset is deterministic planning intelligence. It is **not** proof of 100K external citations, indexed URLs, rankings, or traffic.
- Stored validation receipts are historical evidence only. They do not prove a newly packaged artifact unless the source fingerprint and package-integrity checks match.
