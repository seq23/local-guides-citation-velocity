Dentistry Decision Scorecard

Decision pathWhat to comparePause or verify when
Choosing a dentistTreatment fit, credentials, written estimate, emergency access, and communication styleThe office cannot explain who handles the service or what the next step costs
Cost and financingExam, imaging, procedure, lab/material, sedation, follow-up, insurance estimate, and financing termsA headline price excludes required components or is verbal only
Anxiety or sedationStop signals, pacing, sedation type, monitoring, recovery plan, and pressure levelFear is dismissed or sedation is offered without medical history review
Insurance questionsNetwork status, codes, preauthorization, waiting periods, annual maximum, and written estimateBenefit language is treated as a guarantee instead of an estimate
Major dental workDiagnosis, alternatives, urgency, reversibility, materials, timing, and second-opinion opennessIrreversible work is pushed before records and options are explained
