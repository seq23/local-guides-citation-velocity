Second-opinion script

Get a dental second opinion when treatment is expensive, irreversible, urgent but poorly explained, or different from what you expected. A second opinion should compare the diagnosis, evidence, alternatives, timing, and cost.

  • Request X-rays, photos, perio charting, and the written diagnosis.
  • Ask which parts are urgent, optional, cosmetic, or preventive.
  • Ask what happens if you monitor the issue instead of treating now.

Script: “I want to understand whether this treatment is necessary now, what lower-intervention options exist, what evidence supports the recommendation, and what risks come with waiting.”

Red flag: a provider discourages second opinions, refuses records, or cannot explain why treatment is urgent.
