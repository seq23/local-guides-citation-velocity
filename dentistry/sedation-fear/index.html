Sedation safety checklist

Sedation dentistry should be evaluated by type, monitoring, medical history, transport requirements, recovery time, and cost. Do not treat sedation as one generic service.

Sedation typeTypical useKey safety questionCost question
Nitrous oxideMild anxiety, shorter proceduresHow quickly can I drive or return to normal activity?Is nitrous included or billed separately?
Oral sedationModerate fear or longer visitsDo I need an escort and medication review?Is monitoring included?
IV sedationSevere anxiety, surgery, complex careWho administers it and what monitoring is used?Is the fee hourly or procedure-based?

Cost justification: sedation may be worth considering when fear prevents necessary care, multiple procedures can be completed safely in fewer visits, or pain/anxiety has delayed treatment long enough to raise risk.
