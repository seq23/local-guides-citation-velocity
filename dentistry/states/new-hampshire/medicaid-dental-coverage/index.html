Reviewed source fact

New Hampshire Medicaid dental reimbursement rates on this page are taken from New Hampshire Medicaid Dental Procedure Code Fee Schedule, published by New Hampshire Department of Health and Human Services (NHMMIS), and retrieved 2026-08-26. The schedule is effective 2026-01-01. Every amount shown is what the state pays a dental provider for the procedure. It is not a price a patient pays, and self-pay and insured prices are typically far higher. New Hampshire operates a limited adult dental benefit for adults 21 and over; children's dental care is required federally under EPSDT. Verified 2026-08-26.

Decision questions

New Hampshire publishes its Medicaid dental reimbursement rates in New Hampshire Medicaid Dental Procedure Code Fee Schedule. Those rates are what New Hampshire Medicaid pays a dental provider for each procedure, and they are not prices a patient pays. A self-pay or insured price for the same procedure is normally much higher, so a New Hampshire reimbursement figure cannot answer what a filling or a crown would cost you. It does answer what New Hampshire Medicaid will pay a dentist who accepts it. The figures on this page were retrieved 2026-08-26.

Red flags

  • A New Hampshire claim with no source date

Adult and child dental benefits are set differently in New Hampshire. Children's dental coverage is federally required under EPSDT, so every state must cover medically necessary dental care for eligible children. Adult dental coverage is a state option, which is why it varies so sharply. New Hampshire operates a limited adult dental benefit for adults aged 21 and over. In the state's own words: "Benefits Available for Medicaid-Eligible Adults Ages 21 and over. A. Covered services include: X-rays and examinations; Cleanings; Fillings; Limited gum-related treatment; Tooth extractions and other oral surgeries. C. $1500 yearly limit on dental services, with the exception of preventive services." Confirm your own category before booking, because New Hampshire eligibility groups can differ. Verified 2026-08-26.
