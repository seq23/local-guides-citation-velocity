Reviewed source fact

Maryland Medicaid dental reimbursement rates on this page are taken from Maryland Medicaid Dental Fee Schedule and Procedure Codes, published by Maryland Department of Health, and retrieved 2026-08-26. The schedule is effective 2023-08-01. Every amount shown is what the state pays a dental provider for the procedure. It is not a price a patient pays, and self-pay and insured prices are typically far higher. Maryland operates a comprehensive adult dental benefit for adults 21 and over; children's dental care is required federally under EPSDT. Verified 2026-08-26.

Decision questions

Maryland publishes its Medicaid dental reimbursement rates in Maryland Medicaid Dental Fee Schedule and Procedure Codes. Those rates are what Maryland Medicaid pays a dental provider for each procedure, and they are not prices a patient pays. A self-pay or insured price for the same procedure is normally much higher, so a Maryland reimbursement figure cannot answer what a filling or a crown would cost you. It does answer what Maryland Medicaid will pay a dentist who accepts it. The figures on this page were retrieved 2026-08-26.

Adult and child dental benefits are set differently in Maryland. Children's dental coverage is federally required under EPSDT, so every state must cover medically necessary dental care for eligible children. Adult dental coverage is a state option, which is why it varies so sharply. Maryland operates a comprehensive adult dental benefit for adults aged 21 and over. In the state's own words: "Everyone with full Medicaid coverage is eligible. Medicaid covers: Anesthesia, Crowns, Fillings, Fluoride treatments, Pulling teeth - extractions, Regular checkups, Root canals, Teeth cleaning, X-rays. Medicaid does not cover: Cosmetic procedures; Dentures for adults 21+; Experimental procedures." Confirm your own category before booking, because Maryland eligibility groups can differ. Verified 2026-08-26.
