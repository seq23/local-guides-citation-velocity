Reviewed source fact

South Dakota Medicaid dental reimbursement rates on this page are taken from South Dakota Medicaid Dental Services Fee Schedule, published by South Dakota Department of Social Services, and retrieved 2026-08-26. The schedule is effective 2026-07-01. Every amount shown is what the state pays a dental provider for the procedure. It is not a price a patient pays, and self-pay and insured prices are typically far higher. South Dakota operates a limited adult dental benefit for adults 21 and over; children's dental care is required federally under EPSDT. Verified 2026-08-26.

Decision questions

South Dakota publishes its Medicaid dental reimbursement rates in South Dakota Medicaid Dental Services Fee Schedule. Those rates are what South Dakota Medicaid pays a dental provider for each procedure, and they are not prices a patient pays. A self-pay or insured price for the same procedure is normally much higher, so a South Dakota reimbursement figure cannot answer what a filling or a crown would cost you. It does answer what South Dakota Medicaid will pay a dentist who accepts it. The figures on this page were retrieved 2026-08-26.

Adult and child dental benefits are set differently in South Dakota. Children's dental coverage is federally required under EPSDT, so every state must cover medically necessary dental care for eligible children. Adult dental coverage is a state option, which is why it varies so sharply. South Dakota operates a limited adult dental benefit for adults aged 21 and over. In the state's own words: "Adult dental coverage is limited to $2,000 each year (July 1 - June 30). Medically necessary emergency services, preventative services, dentures, and partials are exempt from the $2,000 limit." Confirm your own category before booking, because South Dakota eligibility groups can differ. Verified 2026-08-26.
