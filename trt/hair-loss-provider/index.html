Quick checklist

  • PRP: ask what evidence the clinic uses, how many sessions are typical, and who is actually a fit
  • Microneedling: ask needle depth, frequency, and whether minoxidil combination efficacy is discussed
  • Medications: ask when topical or oral medication should come before procedures
  • Timeline: ask what change is realistic in 3, 6, and 12 months
  • Verdict: ask what the clinic thinks is the best first move and why

Red flags

  • Provider cannot separate hormonal vs aesthetic hair loss
  • Promises regrowth without a timeline
  • Pushes procedure before basic workup
  • No verdict on why one option fits better
  • No evidence discussion for PRP or microneedling
