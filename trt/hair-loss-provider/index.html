PRP vs Microneedling for Hair Loss

FactorPRPMicroneedling
What it isPlatelet-rich plasma injections from your own bloodControlled scalp needling to stimulate repair pathways
Best fitEarly thinning with clinician-guided treatment planMild-to-moderate thinning, often paired with topical therapy
Typical cadenceSeries of office sessions, then maintenanceRepeated sessions; protocol depends on depth and supervision
Main limitationCost and inconsistent responseTechnique-sensitive; irritation risk if overdone

Verdict: PRP is usually the more clinic-driven option; microneedling is more protocol-sensitive and often works best as part of a broader hair-loss plan.
