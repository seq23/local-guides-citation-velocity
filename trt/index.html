Quick checklist

  • Confirm baseline labs before treatment
  • Ask who reviews labs and adjusts dosing
  • Compare monitoring cadence and side-effect protocols
  • Ask about fertility, hair-loss risk, sleep apnea, blood pressure, and hematocrit
  • Compare total monthly cost including labs, medication, supplies, and visits
  • Avoid guaranteed-results or treatment-without-labs claims
  • Confirm licensed medical oversight and baseline labs
  • Ask how follow-up labs and side effects are monitored
  • Ask how fertility and hair-loss risks are discussed before treatment
  • Compare total monthly cost, medication cost, visit fees, and refill process

Red flags

  • Best clinic claims without monitoring detail
  • No written lab or follow-up plan
