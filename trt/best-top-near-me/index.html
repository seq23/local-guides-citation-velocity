How to Choose a TRT Provider

  • Labs first: Confirm baseline labs before any prescription.
  • Monitoring: Ask how often follow-up labs and dose reviews happen.
  • Fertility: Ask about sperm banking, HCG, and family-planning tradeoffs.
  • Pricing: Get medication, lab, visit, and supply costs in writing.
  • Safety: Confirm how hematocrit, PSA, sleep apnea, and side effects are monitored.
