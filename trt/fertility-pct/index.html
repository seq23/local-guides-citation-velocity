TRT and Fertility: What to Ask Before Starting

  • Sperm banking: Ask whether it should be considered before TRT.
  • HCG or fertility-preserving options: Ask whether alternatives or adjuncts fit your situation.
  • Recovery timeline: Ask what happens if you stop TRT later to try for pregnancy.
  • Baseline testing: Ask whether semen analysis or reproductive labs are appropriate.

Bottom line: Fertility should be discussed before TRT starts, not after side effects appear.
