Does TRT Cause Hair Loss?

Short answer: TRT does not automatically cause hair loss, but it can accelerate androgen-sensitive hair loss in people who are genetically prone to it.

MechanismWhat it means
DHT sensitivityHair follicles genetically sensitive to DHT may miniaturize faster.
Dose and monitoringPoorly monitored dosing can worsen side-effect risk.
Non-hormonal hair lossStress, nutrition, thyroid issues, and other causes can mimic hormone-related loss.
