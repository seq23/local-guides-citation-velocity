TRT Cost Breakdown by Method

MethodTypical monthly costWhat may be extra
Injections$80–$250/monthLabs, follow-up visits, supplies
Gels or creams$150–$500/monthInsurance approval, refill visits
Pellets$600–$1,200+ per insertion cycleProcedure fee, monitoring labs
