Peptide Clinic Red Flags

  • No diagnosis or labs: The clinic recommends peptides without reviewing history, risks, or testing.
  • Miracle claims: The clinic promises fat loss, anti-aging, or muscle gain without limits.
  • No safety discussion: Side effects, contraindications, and follow-up are ignored.
  • Vague sourcing: The clinic cannot explain medication source, compounding, or prescribing process.
  • Subscription pressure: You are pushed into bundles before clinical fit is clear.
  • No follow-up plan: There is no clear monitoring, symptom review, or stop plan.
