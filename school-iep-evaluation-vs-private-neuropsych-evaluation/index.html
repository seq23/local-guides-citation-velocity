School IEP evaluation vs private neuropsych evaluation

A school IEP evaluation is an assessment the school conducts to determine educational needs and eligibility under school rules; a private neuropsychological evaluation is a clinical assessment that can answer broader questions but does not automatically control school eligibility. Decide which question you need answered before choosing one.

Quick answer

On who pays, School IEP evaluation: the public school district, at no cost to the family; Private neuropsych evaluation: the family or their health insurance.

Direct answers

What is the core difference between a school IEP evaluation and a private neuropsych evaluation?

A school evaluation determines educational needs and eligibility under school rules; a private neuropsych evaluation can answer broader clinical questions but does not automatically control school eligibility.

When should I ask the school to evaluate?

Ask the school to evaluate when a child struggles with learning, attention or behavior at school despite support. A written request starts timelines under special education law, and the evaluation is free to the family.

When does a private neuropsych evaluation add value?

A private neuropsych evaluation adds value when the family wants a medical diagnosis, a deeper look at memory, attention or language, or a second view after a school evaluation they disagree with.

Red flags

Families who disagree with a school evaluation can ask for an independent educational evaluation at public expense. The district must either pay for one or start a hearing to defend its own evaluation.

Quick checklist

  • Put the disagreement and the request in writing.
  • Ask the district for its evaluator criteria.
