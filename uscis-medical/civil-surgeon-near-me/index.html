How to Find a Civil Surgeon Near You

  • Use the USCIS civil surgeon locator to confirm approval.
  • Confirm availability and pricing before booking.
  • Compare multiple providers instead of choosing only the closest listing.
  • Ask how I-693 corrections are handled after the visit.
