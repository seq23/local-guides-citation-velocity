How to Choose a Civil Surgeon (USCIS Medical Exam)

Fast decision: USCIS.gov confirms which doctors are approved. This checklist helps you choose the right one.

  • USCIS approval: Confirm they are a designated civil surgeon.
  • Price clarity: Ask what is included vs separate: labs, vaccines, and form completion.
  • Timeline: Ask how long I-693 takes to complete.
  • Document requirements: Confirm what to bring.
  • Correction policy: Ask how mistakes are handled.
