How to start the USCIS medical exam process the right way

Direct answer: choose a USCIS civil surgeon by comparing designation status, total out-of-pocket cost, document handling, vaccine/lab policy, appointment timing, and sealed I-693 handoff—not by distance alone. If it’s urgent, prioritize availability today and clear next steps before you commit. For USCIS medical exams, the key is a qualified civil surgeon and correct paperwork.
