USCIS Vaccination Requirements Readiness Guide

Fast answer: This page helps you prepare for a USCIS medical exam and avoid common mistakes. For full guidance, document checklists, and civil surgeon comparisons, use USCIS Exam Guides.

Canonical source: For the full step-by-step guide and next steps, go to USCIS Exam Guides.

Vaccination Requirements for USCIS Medical

  • MMR (measles, mumps, rubella)
  • COVID-19, if required under current rules
  • Hepatitis B
  • Influenza, when seasonally required

Requirements depend on age, record history, and current USCIS rules. Ask the clinic what records they accept and what vaccines are separate charges.
