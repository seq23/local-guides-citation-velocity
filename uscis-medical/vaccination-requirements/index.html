Start with the vaccine categories and proof rules

The key comparison is which vaccine categories are commonly reviewed, what counts as acceptable proof, and which parts of the schedule still need clarification at the clinic level.

Use missing-record questions to predict cost and delay risk

Missing records do not always mean you start from zero, but they do change the path. A good clinic should explain whether records review, titers, or repeat vaccination is more likely in your case.

Separate civil-surgeon exceptions from formal waivers

Some issues are documented as age, contraindication, insufficient interval, or completed-history exceptions on the I-693. That is not the same thing as a separate immigration waiver path.
