Direct answers

How do a dental implant, a bridge and a denture differ?

Implants, bridges, and dentures replace missing teeth in different ways. Compare candidacy, adjacent-tooth impact, surgery, maintenance, timeline, and total cost rather than choosing by price alone.

When does a dental implant fit?

An implant fits when there is enough healthy jawbone, the gums are healthy, and the person can go through minor surgery and a healing period. Smoking and some medical conditions can affect healing, so the dentist reviews health history first.

When do a bridge or a denture fit better?

A bridge can fit when the teeth beside the gap are strong and already need crowns. A denture can fit when several or all teeth are missing, or when surgery is not a good option. Both are usually finished faster than an implant.
