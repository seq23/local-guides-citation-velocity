Cosmetic dentistry vs general dentistry

General dentistry is dental care focused on prevention, diagnosis and treatment of oral health problems; cosmetic dentistry is care aimed mainly at the appearance of the teeth and smile. Many procedures overlap and still require a clinical assessment, so compare the diagnosis, alternatives and written estimate before choosing.

Direct answers

What is the core difference between cosmetic and general dentistry?

General dentistry focuses on prevention, diagnosis, and oral health treatment; cosmetic dentistry focuses on appearance, although many procedures overlap and still require clinical assessment.

When does cosmetic dentistry fit?

Cosmetic dentistry fits when the teeth are healthy and the goal is to change color, shape or alignment for appearance. It works best after decay or gum disease has been treated, since cosmetic work placed over untreated problems can fail.

When should I start with general dentistry?

Start with general dentistry when there is pain, a broken tooth, bleeding gums, or it has been a long time since a checkup. A general dentist diagnoses and treats health problems first and can refer for cosmetic or specialty care.
