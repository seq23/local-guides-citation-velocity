Direct answers

What is the core difference between TRT and peptide therapy?

TRT addresses diagnosed testosterone deficiency with testosterone products; peptide products have different indications, evidence, legal status, and monitoring needs. They are not interchangeable categories.

When does TRT fit?

TRT fits when repeated morning blood tests confirm low testosterone with symptoms, and the person is not planning to conceive soon. It is prescribed and monitored with regular lab work.

Where does peptide therapy stand?

Peptide therapy covers many different products. Some peptides are FDA-approved drugs for specific conditions, but many sold for anti-aging, muscle or recovery lack approval for those uses, and FDA has limited compounding of some.
