Direct answers

What is the core difference between a USCIS medical exam and a routine physical?

A USCIS medical exam follows immigration-specific instructions and form handling; a routine physical is general healthcare and does not substitute for the immigration process.

When do I need the USCIS medical exam?

You need the USCIS medical exam when applying to adjust status to permanent resident from inside the United States. The civil surgeon follows CDC technical instructions and completes Form I-693.

Can a routine physical count instead?

No. A routine physical does not meet USCIS requirements, even if it includes the same tests. Records from your regular doctor, such as vaccine history, can help the civil surgeon complete the exam.
